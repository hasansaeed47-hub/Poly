Subject: NOTICE TO SHAREHOLDERS – EXTENSION OF TENURE OF PRESIDENT & CEO

Dear Shareholder,

We are pleased to inform you that the Board of Directors of The Bank of
Punjab ("the Bank") has approved a one (1) year extension in the tenure
of Mr. Zafar Masud as President & Chief Executive Officer of the Bank,
effective April 16, 2026.

This decision was taken at the Board's 330th Emergent Meeting on April 13,
2026, following the recommendation and concurrence of the Finance Department,
Government of the Punjab.

Mr. Masud has served as President & CEO of The Bank of Punjab since March
2020. During his tenure, the Bank has witnessed significant improvements in
its financial performance, asset quality, governance standards, and digital
capabilities.

Key achievements under his leadership include:

  - Consistent growth in net income and improvement in return on equity
  - Reduction in non-performing loans (NPLs) and improved provision coverage
  - Successful launch and expansion of digital banking products
  - Enhanced capital adequacy and liquidity ratios
  - Strengthening of the Bank's compliance and risk culture
  - Expansion of branch network and financial inclusion outreach

The Board believes that Mr. Masud's continued leadership will further
strengthen the Bank's competitive position and deliver enhanced value to
all shareholders.

A formal notification has been filed with the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX)
and the relevant regulatory authorities in accordance with applicable legal
and regulatory requirements.
