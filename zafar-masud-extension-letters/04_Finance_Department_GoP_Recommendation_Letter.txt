Subject: RECOMMENDATION FOR EXTENSION IN TENURE OF MR. ZAFAR MASUD
         AS PRESIDENT & CEO, THE BANK OF PUNJAB

Dear Chairman,

I write with reference to the matter of the impending expiry of the employment
contract of Mr. Zafar Masud, President & Chief Executive Officer of The Bank
of Punjab, on April 15, 2026.

The Government of the Punjab, being the principal shareholder of The Bank of
Punjab, has carefully considered the matter and reviewed the performance of
Mr. Zafar Masud during his tenure as President & CEO since March 2020.

Having regard to:

  (i)   The significant turnaround and overall improvement in the financial
        health and operational performance of The Bank of Punjab under
        Mr. Masud's leadership;

  (ii)  His demonstrated commitment to strengthening corporate governance,
        risk management, and regulatory compliance;

  (iii) His contribution to the Bank's digital transformation agenda and
        financial inclusion objectives aligned with the Government's priorities;

  (iv)  The stability and continuity of leadership that is essential for
        the Bank at this juncture of its ongoing transformation;

  (v)   His recent election as Chairman of the Pakistan Banks Association
        (PBA), which further attests to the confidence of the broader
        banking sector in his capabilities;

The Finance Department, Government of the Punjab, hereby recommends and
concurs with the extension of Mr. Zafar Masud's employment contract as
President & Chief Executive Officer of The Bank of Punjab for a further
period of one (1) year, effective April 16, 2026, on his existing terms
and conditions.

The Board of Directors is accordingly requested to take necessary action
for formal approval and regulatory compliance in this regard.
