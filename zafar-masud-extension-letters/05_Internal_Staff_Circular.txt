                           INTERNAL CIRCULAR

Circular No: BOP/HR/2026/___                                  April 16, 2026

TO   : All Employees – The Bank of Punjab (Head Office, Regional Offices,
       Branch Network, and all departments/divisions)
FROM : Human Resources Division, Head Office
RE   : EXTENSION OF TENURE OF PRESIDENT & CEO – MR. ZAFAR MASUD

Dear Colleagues,

We are pleased to share the exciting news that the Board of Directors of
The Bank of Punjab, at its 330th Emergent Board Meeting held on April 13,
2026, has approved a one (1) year extension in the tenure of our esteemed
President & Chief Executive Officer, Mr. Zafar Masud, effective April 16, 2026.

This decision by the Board and the Government of the Punjab reflects the
confidence placed in Mr. Masud's visionary leadership and the remarkable
progress The Bank of Punjab has made under his guidance over the past six
years.

Under Mr. Masud's stewardship, our Bank has undergone a remarkable
transformation:

  * Improved financial performance and profitability
  * Strengthened governance and risk management culture
  * Expansion of our digital banking services and innovation agenda
  * Enhanced customer experience and service delivery standards
  * Growth of branch network and financial inclusion initiatives
  * Improved employee welfare and capacity building programs
  * Restored credibility and trust among stakeholders

We are confident that Mr. Masud's continued leadership will bring further
success, growth, and excellence to our institution.

We request all employees to extend their fullest support and cooperation
to our President & CEO as we continue our journey of excellence and service.

Please join us in congratulating Mr. Zafar Masud on this well-deserved
extension and wish him continued success.

For THE BANK OF PUNJAB

Group Head – Human Resources
