Dear Governor,

In compliance with the applicable directives of the State Bank of Pakistan
and the relevant provisions of the Banking Companies Ordinance, 1962, we
hereby notify your esteemed office of the following:

The Board of Directors of The Bank of Punjab, at its 330th Emergent Board
Meeting held on April 13, 2026, has formally approved a one (1) year
extension in the employment contract of Mr. Zafar Masud as President &
Chief Executive Officer of The Bank of Punjab, effective April 16, 2026,
on his existing terms and conditions of employment.

PROFILE SUMMARY:
Mr. Zafar Masud is a seasoned banking professional with over three decades
of experience in domestic and international banking. He holds an MBA and

(PBA).
