RESOLUTION: EXTENSION IN TENURE OF PRESIDENT & CEO

WHEREAS, the employment contract of Mr. Zafar Masud, President & Chief
Executive Officer, The Bank of Punjab, is due to expire on April 15, 2026;

WHEREAS, under Mr. Zafar Masud's leadership since March 2020, The Bank of
Punjab has demonstrated significant growth in its financial performance,
improved asset quality, expanded its digital banking footprint, and
strengthened its governance framework;

WHEREAS, the Finance Department, Government of Punjab has conveyed its
concurrence for the extension of Mr. Zafar Masud's tenure as President & CEO
of The Bank of Punjab;

WHEREAS, the Board of Directors has evaluated the performance of Mr. Zafar
Masud and finds it in the best interest of the Bank and its stakeholders to
continue his leadership;

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:

1. The Board of Directors hereby approves and confirms a one (1) year
   extension in the employment contract of Mr. Zafar Masud as President &
   Chief Executive Officer of The Bank of Punjab, effective April 16, 2026,
   on the existing terms and conditions of his employment agreement.

2. The Company Secretary is hereby authorized and directed to notify the
   Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited (PSX), the Securities & Exchange
   Commission of Pakistan (SECP), and the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) of
   this resolution as required under applicable laws and regulations.

3. All concerned departments are directed to take necessary administrative
   actions to implement this resolution.
